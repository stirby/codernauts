# Future leaderboard ideas

Current scope has exactly one leaderboard: season gravel. See `docs/mvp/scaling.md` for why one number wins and why runaway leaders are telemetry, not a problem.

## Category boards (the back pocket)

If the single gravelboard ever gets stale, add category boards as secondary views, never as the main ranking. Earlier candidates:

- Fastest first automation.
- Most ore generated during a season.
- Fewest API calls to reach a goal.
- Most reliable bot over a fixed window.
- Best starter-client extension or dashboard.

## Trophies and archives

Later seasons may want permanent records that survive resets:

- Season winner trophies on player profiles.
- Archived final boards per season.
- Community reporting, such as end-of-season summaries.

Guardrail: scoring should reward programming skill and strategy, not workspace spend or brute-force polling.
